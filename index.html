GameDevTool: Free Roblox Creator Calculators

A small index of free Roblox creator calculators I use when pricing game passes and planning DevEx cash-outs. Each one runs in the browser with no login.

  • Roblox Tax Calculator: what you receive after the 30% fee, and what to list at to net an exact amount.
  • Roblox DevEx Calculator: earned Robux to USD at the $0.0038, $0.0035 and US 18+ $0.0054 rates, with the 30,000 minimum.
  • Robux to USD Calculator: what Robux cost to buy versus what they pay out.
  • UGC Revenue Share Calculator: your 30% to 70% share, based on price versus the floor.
  • Limited Resale Calculator: seller, creator, affiliate and Roblox splits.
